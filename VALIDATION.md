## Limits

These checks are not a performance benchmark, a complete website-compatibility suite, a security audit, or proof of identical Arc behavior. A deterministic gesture test does not measure a physical trackpad's timing or haptic feel. Component tests and controller fixtures validate different boundaries; neither substitutes for the other.

PiP uses a Bam-owned floating panel and HTML5-video isolation. Cross-origin embeds, protected players, and arbitrary site-specific media controls are not established as supported. Apple Silicon is the release target; Intel hardware and older macOS releases are unverified. Signing is local/ad-hoc; notarization and secure update delivery are not implemented. See [README.md](README.md) and [FEATURE-INVENTORY.md](FEATURE-INVENTORY.md) for the implemented subsets and remaining work.
